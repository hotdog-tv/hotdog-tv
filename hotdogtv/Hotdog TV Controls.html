Rewind & catch-up

Turn on Settings › Timeshift › Enable Timeshift (it's off to begin with) and live channels are buffered while you watch, so you can pause and rewind. Channels with catch-up can also go back to earlier programmes from the TV Guide. Which buttons seek is set under Seeking Options in Settings › Remote Control.

Live channel with Timeshift on

ButtonWhat it does
⏪
Rewind, with the seek bar on screen. Going past the start of the buffer carries on into catch-up where the channel has it.
⏩
Fast-forward (only once you're behind live).
⏯
Pause / play, with the seek bar on screen.
◀ ▶
Skip back / forward, but only after you've rewound or paused. At live they do their normal job.
Hold⏯
Back to live.

ButtonWhat it does

▲ ▼
Channel preview as usual. Can be set to seek instead.

With Timeshift off, ⏪ on a catch-up channel rewinds from live into catch-up.

Multiview

Up to four channels at once. Start it from Multiview in the menu bar, or Add to Multiview in the TV Guide's programme menu. The screen with the blue frame is the one your buttons control.

ButtonWhat it does
▲ ▼ ◀ ▶
Move between screens. Sound follows the highlighted screen.
OK
On a smaller screen: make it the main one. On the main screen: open the screen menu.
HoldOK Menu
Screen menu: change channel, layout, Watch full screen, Close Multiview and more.
CH+ CH−
Next / previous channel on the highlighted screen.
Back
TV Guide. Multiview stays running; leave it from the screen menu.
⏯ ⏪ ⏩ ⏹
On the channel you started from: pause, rewind and fast-forward as usual; Stop goes back to live.
Defaults for Hotdog TV 0.9.0-beta1 on Android TV. If you've changed buttons in Settings › Remote Control, yours will differ.
