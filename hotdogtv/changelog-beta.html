Hotdog TV — What's New

0.14.2 beta

New

  • Multiview up to nine channels — on boxes fast enough to play them all. The box's video decoder says how many it can manage: one that plays 4K at 60 frames a second gets nine, slower ones fewer, and a box that doesn't say gets four, as before.
  • More Multiview layouts — hold OK (or press OK on the main screen) and choose Change layout to see the layouts for the number of channels you have open, each with a small picture of itself. New ones include Main + side column (one big screen with the others stacked down the right, all lined up, and the seek bar in the black band underneath), Main + L, and grids for six, eight and nine. Every screen keeps the shape of the picture, so nothing is stretched.

Improved

  • Four channels in Multiview now start as Main + side column — Grid and Main on top are still in Change layout, and a layout you chose before is kept.
  • Silo watched and progress, both ways — what you watch, finish or mark as watched or unwatched in Silo's own apps now shows in Hotdog TV within a few minutes (straight away when you come back to the app or open Movies and Shows), not only after a full update. Unwatched or removed in Silo now clears here too. Anything that didn't reach Silo from Hotdog TV (the server was down, say) is sent the next time. Hotdog Channels still never count as watched.

0.14.1 beta

New

  • Back to the top of the groups list — hold Left in the groups list (TV Guide, Movies and Shows) to jump to the top of the playlist you're in. Hold it again to go to the top of the whole list. A short press of Left still goes to the side bar.

Improved

  • Faster Silo updates — updating a Silo server no longer rewrites your whole library each time. Silo renews its poster links every few hours, which made every update look like a new library. Now only what has really changed is saved, and renewed posters are written on their own. The update shows as "Checking films" and the status says "no changes" when nothing changed.

0.14.0 beta

New

  • Show / Hide Channels — hold OK on a group in the groups list and pick Show / Hide Channels: the group's channels are listed, hidden ones too, and you tick the ones you want. It's in the TV Guide too: hold OK on a channel, then Hide › Show / Hide Channels. Untick every channel and the whole group is hidden instead, so Show / Hide Groups can bring it back.
  • Select all and deselect all — in Show / Hide Channels and Show / Hide Groups, Right ticks every row and Left unticks every row, as in Manage Favourites. Back saves and finishes.
  • Hotdog Channels — My Channels is now called Hotdog Channels. Manage Hotdog Channels in Settings opens a new page laid out like Recordings: your channels down the left, and the chosen one on the right with what's on now and next, its films and series, and its options.
  • Play in External Player — hold OK on a film or episode on the Movies and Shows home page, in search results, or on a finished recording to play it in another player app, such as VLC.
  • Choose the player's menu buttons — Settings › Appearance › Player › Menu: hide the buttons you don't use in the menu over the player, and put the rest in the order you like.

Improved

  • Menus rearranged — Reminders is in the side bar. The player's menu has Multiview, Recordings and Reminders; My List and picture-in-picture have gone from it, and My List from the TV Guide's hold-OK menu. Add to Channel is now Add a Hotdog Channel.
  • Settings › Playlists & EPG — Playlists and EPG are together, with Channels Sorting and Groups Sorting under Playlists. Moved Films & Series is under Notifications.

0.13.12 beta

New

  • Grid layout for three screens in multiview — with three channels on screen, the screen menu (hold OK on a screen) now has Layout: grid, which makes all three the same size, two on top and one below. Layout: main on top puts it back.
  • Radio from Movies, Shows and Recordings — the side bar there, and a film's Back menu, have Radio under Live TV. It opens the TV Guide on the radio stations.
  • Preview Mode for the Channels list — Settings › Appearance › Player › Channels List › Preview Mode: the Channels tile in the info bar opens the list with the channel playing in a window beside it.
  • Show Pop-ups — Settings › Notifications › Show Pop-ups. Turn it off and subscription reminders no longer pop up. New ones are counted on the Settings icon instead.

Fixed

  • Left after changing channel — with the info bar up after a channel change, Left now goes back to the previous channel (or whatever you've set Left to do) instead of opening the Channels list.

0.13.11 beta

New

  • Reload in multiview — the screen menu (hold OK on a screen) has Reload: that screen connects to its channel again, and the others carry on.

0.13.10

New

  • Subscription reminders — when an Xtream subscription (or an M3U playlist on an Xtream account) ends within 7 days, a notice pops up in the top right corner, once. Settings › Notifications lists every subscription ending within 30 days.
  • Move the small screen in multiview — in picture-in-picture, the screen menu's Move small screen lets you put it in any of nine places with the arrows. OK or Back keeps it there.
  • Jellyfin and Silo from the sync portal — add a Jellyfin or Silo server once in the sync portal (Playlists › Add playlist, Type: Jellyfin server or Silo server) and every TV on your account signs itself in to it. With a Silo account that has more than one profile, each TV asks which one the first time you open Movies or Shows. Needs sync server 0.5.14.
  • Choose several recent channels — hold OK on a recent channel in the info bar and pick Select Channels. Tick the ones you want with OK, then press Up for the buttons above the channels: Multiview to watch them all together, Remove to take them off your recents, or Cancel. Back stops choosing too.

Fixed

  • Hidden groups in Movies and Shows — films and series from a group you've hidden no longer turn up on the Movies and Shows home page (Continue Watching, Next Up, New Series, Favourites, Recently Added).
  • Every Xtream channel saying "not found" — when the server address was saved with a slash on the end (`https://server:port/`), the app asked for channels at `server:port//live/…`, which providers turn away. The extra slash is now left out, and films, episodes, catch-up and recordings saved before the fix work too.
  • Xtream channels that play in other apps but not here — when a provider turns a channel's address away, the app now tries it as MPEG-TS, then the stream server the provider names when you sign in, then the channel's own direct address where the provider gives one, before giving up. Whichever plays is used for that channel from then on.
  • Steadier scrolling in the TV Guide and the groups list — moving up and down, the white box used to jump a row ahead for a moment before the list caught up with it. It now stays put and the list moves under it.

0.13.4

Fixed

  • A sharper logo — the Hotdog TV logo in the side bar, on the setup screen and on the home screen banner is redrawn from the original artwork: the letters line up again, and it's no longer blocky on the TV.

0.13.3 beta

New

  • Connect with your sync code — Quick Connect is gone. On a new TV, choose I have a sync code and type your sync code (eight letters and numbers) shown on the sync portal's Dashboard or in Settings › Sync on another TV. That's all it takes.
  • Make an account from the TV — when the sync server allows it, New here? › Create a new sync code makes your account right there. If the server's owner approves new accounts first, the TV waits and carries on by itself once they do.
  • Your code in Settings — Settings › Sync shows your account and its sync code. Add a Portal Login gives you a QR code to choose a username and password on your phone, to manage your account on the web.

Improved

  • New accounts start with a playlist — when the account you connect to has no playlists yet, setup goes on to adding one, and it's kept on your account.

Making an account from the TV needs sync server 0.5.13. Older servers still connect with the sync code.

0.13.2 beta

Fixed

  • My Channels and Silo — watching a My Channel no longer shows up in Silo's watch history, and isn't passed on to Trakt or Simkl as you watching all those bits of shows. Films and episodes you watch yourself still sync your place as before.

0.13.1 beta

New

  • Resume channels — a My Channel can now pick up where you left off instead of running on like live TV. Leave Harbour Lights at S1 E6, 23 minutes in, and when you come back it plays S1 E6 from 23 minutes; when an episode ends, the next one starts. Settings › Playlists › My Channels › (a channel) › When You Come Back: Live or Resume.
  • Episode numbers in the TV guide — programmes in the guide now show their season and episode, laid out the way you chose in Settings › Appearance › TV Guide › Episode Details. Turn it off there with Show in the Guide Grid.
  • One-series channels — a My Channel with just one series shows only the episode (S1 E6) in the guide, since the channel already says which series it is.

Improved

  • Adding a playlist or server in Settings no longer covers the screen until it's done. It carries on in the background, with its progress in Settings › Playlists (like a TV guide update), and you can keep using the menu or close it. With Settings closed, a small card in the top right corner shows how it's going and says when it's added.

0.13.0 beta

New

  • My Channels — make your own 24/7 channels from your films and series. Hold OK on a film or series in Movies & Shows (or use the button on its page) › Add to Channel, and pick a channel or make a new one. Mix series and films in one channel.
  • A TV guide that keeps going — My Channels have their own guide, episode by episode. Tune in and you join part-way through, like live TV; close the app and come back days later and it's where the guide says, not back at the first episode.
  • Choose the order — Settings › Playlists › My Channels: take turns (an episode of each series in turn), one series after another, or shuffle. Rename a channel, move or remove what's in it, or include specials. Changes show from the next programme.
  • On every box — with Sync, your channels go to your other devices, and the sync portal shows what's on now and next. Watching a channel doesn't change Continue Watching or what's marked as watched.

Both rows now count what you watched in any version.

0.12.16

New

  • Silo with profiles — type the address you use for Silo in a browser and the app signs in with Silo itself: approve a code from your phone (or scan it), or use your Silo username and password. Then choose who's watching; a profile with a PIN asks for it. Films, series, where you got to and what's watched are that profile's, and Settings › Playlists › (the Silo playlist) › Profile switches to another. Silo also changes what this box can't play into something it can.
  • Find your Jellyfin or Silo server — Add Jellyfin or Silo (in Settings and in first-run setup) searches your network and lists the servers it finds; pick one and its address is filled in.
  • One title, every version — a film or series that's in more than one playlist (your provider, Jellyfin, Silo), or that your provider has in 4K or Dolby Vision as well, now shows once in Movies and Shows. Its page has a version button beside Play listing each one, such as "VOD · 4K Dolby Vision" or "Silo"; pick one and the app remembers it for that title. Where you got to and what's watched carry across versions, episode by episode.
  • Play asks which version — on a title with more than one version, Play asks "Play from" with each one listed. The one it would play is first and ticked, so OK twice plays it. Turn it off in Settings › Playback › Ask Which Version to Play.
  • Playlist button in Movies and Shows — beside Search (top right): show one playlist's films or series, or all of them.
  • Movie & Series Quality and Source — in Settings › Playback. Quality starts at "Best This TV Can Show"; Source picks the playlist to play from when a title is in several.
  • Record what follows — choose Record on a programme with others straight after it and you can carry on through them: pick how far ("until 15:00", "until 16:00"…) and each programme is saved as its own recording, recorded back to back on one connection. Made for sport, where the guide splits a match into the build-up, the game and what comes after.
  • Recordings remember where you stopped — a recording (or a catch-up download) carries on from where you left it, as films and episodes do.

Fixed

  • Two recordings in a row — the second of two recordings back to back could stop after about half a minute while the box was asleep. The app now stays awake between them, and recordings of the same channel share one connection, so the second begins with no gap.
  • Stop recording keeps the recording — on a programme being recorded, the menu now says Stop recording and keeps what was recorded.
  • Recordings that finished on time were still shown as recording until the app was next opened.
  • A recording showing on the wrong channel — channels that share a TV guide (a channel's HD and UHD) all showed the red recording mark when only one was being recorded. Each recording now belongs to the channel it was made on.
  • Skipping ahead in a recording — after rewinding, skipping forward could jump back to the start of the recording.
  • Multiview Change channel and Add screen — choosing a channel in the TV Guide after either changed the main screen. It now goes on the screen you chose, or is added as a new one.
  • Deleted playlists leave Continue Watching — films and episodes from a playlist you've deleted or switched off no longer stay in Continue Watching or Next Up.
  • Silo's web address — typing the address you use for Silo in a browser said it wasn't a Jellyfin or Silo server.
  • Films and episodes with only Jellyfin or Silo — starting an episode from its page could jump to Movies while it began playing behind it.
  • Problem reports cover more time — a stream full of broken packets filled the report's log in a few minutes. Those warnings are now counted on one line.

0.12.13

Fixed

  • Crashing a minute after starting, over and over — on a 2 GB box (the Shield) with a very big Movies & Series list, the update after a provider rebuilt its library ran out of memory and the app closed. The update was lost, so the next start tried again and closed again. Finding favourites and Continue Watching their new numbers now uses far less memory, and the app may use more of the box's memory when it needs to.

New

  • Jellyfin and Silo — add your own media server's films and series: Settings › Playlists › Add Playlist › Add Jellyfin or Silo (or Jellyfin or Silo in first-run setup). Sign in with Quick Connect (the TV shows a code to approve from a phone or computer) or a username and password. They appear in Movies & Shows with their posters, details and episodes, and play the file as it is on the server. Where you got to goes back to the server, so other Jellyfin apps carry on from there, and what you watched in them shows here. Silo connects through its Jellyfin-compatible address (films and series only; for a Silo profile, sign in as name#Profile).
  • Films and series only? With no live channels at all, the app opens in Movies (or Shows) instead of an empty TV guide.
  • Quick Connect for Sync — in first-run setup and Settings › Sync, the TV shows a 6-digit code: type it into the sync portal's Dashboard (or scan the QR code) and the TV is paired, no sync code to copy. Needs Sync Server 0.5.9.
  • Customise in setup — after signing in with Sync, choose Customise to set this TV's own look and guide, playback, remote and parental controls, starting from your shared settings or the defaults. What you change becomes this TV's own; everything else keeps following your other devices.

0.12.12

Fixed

  • Favourites and Continue Watching survive a provider's rebuild — when a provider rebuilds its Movies or Series, every film, series and episode gets a new number, and favourites, Continue Watching, Next Up and watched ticks disappeared. They now follow each film or series to its new number, matched by its TMDB id or, failing that, by a title only one item has. Films and series lost this way before this version are found again once every playlist has loaded.

New

0.12.11

New

  • Parental controls — Settings › Parental Controls, as in TiviMate: a 4-digit PIN for blocked channels and groups (hold OK on one and choose Block), and — your choice — for Settings, its Playlists and EPG pages, and the channel and group menus. A blocked channel shows a padlock instead of the picture: press OK and enter the PIN. Choose how long an unlock lasts, and whether the PIN is typed with number keys or picked with Up/Down.
  • More than one PIN — the Main PIN opens everything. Add other PINs, each opening only what you tick for it: Settings pages, the menus, and which blocked groups and channels it can watch. A PIN that can't open something asks for one that can.

  • Forgotten PIN? With the sync server, set a new Main PIN for the TV in the portal (Settings, choose the device; needs Sync Server 0.5.8). Parental controls are kept on the server as each TV's own, never shared.

Fixed

  • Recent channels wait before adding a channel — Delay Before Adding to Recent is now honoured: a channel only joins Recents once it has stayed on that long, so zapping past channels no longer fills the strip.

0.11.5

Fixed

  • Nine: the picture went black with the sound still playing — Nine sends its channels in several qualities, some at 50 frames a second and some at 25, and the app moves between them as the connection allows. On some boxes (a Xiaomi TV Box S among them) each move also switched the TV's refresh rate between 50 and 25 Hz, until the picture didn't come back. The app now leaves the TV's refresh rate alone unless Settings › Playback › Auto Frame Rate is on.
  • No sound on Nine with Timeshift on — Nine keeps its sound apart from its picture, and timeshift kept only the picture. Nine's channels now play straight from the stream, with sound, though without pause and rewind for now. Recording one says it can't be recorded yet, rather than making a silent recording.

Improved

  • Problem reports — a report sent from a box that syncs with the same server is filed under its person, so their group's admins can read it there.
  • Problem reports say how the box is connected — a new Network part: Wi-Fi or a network cable, the Wi-Fi band, signal strength and link speed, whether a VPN is in the way, and how quickly the last five minutes of the stream came in. A channel that keeps stopping to load can then be put down to the box's connection or to the provider. No network names or addresses are included.

0.11.4

Improved

  • A new logo — Hotdog TV has a new hotdog: on the home screen's app icon and banner, the start-up screen, the menu down the left, and first-time setup. On Android 13 and later the icon can also take your launcher's themed colours.
  • Readable words on the Focus Colour — with Settings › Appearance › Focus Colour set to Theme Colour, the words on whatever has the focus were always black, and hard to read on the darker colour themes. They are now white or black, whichever stands out on your colour.

0.11.3

New

  • Recordings named after the programme — a recording's file is now called what it is, "Harbour Lights - S01E02 - The Long Way.ts", from the title and episode the TV Guide gives. With no episode details it has the date and time it began, so a daily programme doesn't end up with a pile of numbered copies. Recordings made before keep their old names.
  • Where recordings are saved — Settings › Recording has two new choices. Save Recordings (Record and Custom recording): in the recordings folder, or a folder for each recording. Save Series Recordings: in the recordings folder, a folder for each recording, a folder for the series, or the series' folder and then one for each season, where the guide gives the season. Settings › Playback › Radio Visualisations sets the one to start on, and whether it stays, moves on to the next in order or changes at random every few minutes. Off keeps the black screen.
  • Choose one while you listen — hold Right on a radio station, or Menu › Visuals, for the list. Each one shows behind the list as you move through it; OK keeps it, Back goes back to the one before.
  • About Hotdog TV — Settings › General: the version, and the open-source software Hotdog TV is built with and its licences.

Improved

  • A radio station with Dolby or DTS sound is decoded by Hotdog TV while a visualisation is on, so the picture has the music to follow. With visualisations Off it goes to the TV or receiver as it is, as before. Channels with a picture are not affected.

0.9.9

Fixed

  • No sound on Dolby and DTS streams — on a device that can't decode them, connected to a TV that doesn't take them either, the picture played silently with no audio tracks to choose from. Hotdog TV now decodes Dolby Digital, Dolby Digital Plus, DTS and Dolby TrueHD itself when nothing else can. Where the TV or a receiver already takes them, nothing changes: they are still passed through as they are.

Improved

  • Stream Info shows how the sound is handled — passed through to the TV, or which decoder is doing it.

Dolby and DTS are decoded with FFmpeg 6.0 (ffmpeg.org), used under the LGPL version 2.1 or later.

0.9.8

New

  • Reminders — hold OK on an upcoming programme in the TV Guide and choose Remind. A bar comes up before it starts with Watch, Remind in 5 min, Record and Dismiss. Settings › Reminders sets how long before, how long the bar waits and what it does if left alone, and lists your reminders.
  • Reminders while Hotdog TV is closed — the bar appears over whatever you're watching, and can wake the box from sleep. Android 10 and newer need "Display over other apps" allowed for Hotdog TV: Settings › Reminders takes you there.
  • Movies and episodes buffer onto storage — several minutes are read ahead onto internal storage, so skipping forward is instant and a slow moment at your provider doesn't stop the film. Playback › Buffer Movies on Storage turns it off.
  • Remove from History — hold OK on a movie or episode in Continue Watching. Next Up has Play, Play in External Player and Remove from Next Up.
  • Play in External Player for episodes — in an episode's hold-OK menu.
  • Favourites from Search — hold OK on a channel, movie or show in the results.
  • Timeshift never fills the drive — Timeshift › Fill Storage Up To: past that, the oldest footage is overwritten.

Improved

  • Back while watching a movie or episode asks first — "Press Back again to stop", so one stray press doesn't end the film.
  • Record explains itself — with recording switched off it says so and opens Settings › Recording, and it tells you when a recording is going to internal storage and how much room is left.
  • A watched season has a tick on its tab.
  • The Favourite button keeps its name; the heart fills in to show it's on.
  • A movie or episode that hits a network error carries on from where it was, not from the beginning.
  • Updates are noticed sooner — Hotdog TV checks when you open it, not only twice a day, and an update you looked at in Settings but didn't install is offered again the next time you open the app.

0.9.7

New

  • Beta versions — Settings › General › Check for Beta Version looks for an early build to try out. Betas are never offered automatically, and nothing installs without asking.

Fixed

  • The app not opening on Android 7 devices, such as the first Fire TV Stick 4K.

0.9.6

Fixed

  • Channels whose address has no extension (such as `…/12345` rather than `…/12345.ts`) failing with "Input does not start with the #EXTM3U header". They play, and timeshift works on them too.
  • Compressed TV guides failing with "Unexpected token" when their address doesn't end in .gz — for example one with a token or a redirect. Zipped guides load too.

0.9.5

New

  • Recent channels menu — hold OK on a recent channel, in the info bar or the sidebar's Recent list: Play, Add to Multiview (info bar), Remove from Recents, or Clear All Recents.
  • Mark a whole season as watched — a button on the series page for the season you're viewing, or hold OK on any season tab to mark it watched or unwatched.

Improved

  • When a playlist's contents change, saving the update no longer slows the rest of the app down.
  • Marking a series or season watched updates in one go and syncs to your other devices straight away.

Fixed

  • Text fields in Settings (such as a playlist's name or address) closing the moment they opened.

0.9.4

New

  • Stream Info — Menu › Stream Info shows what's playing and how well: resolution, frame rate, codec, bitrate, audio, buffer, connection speed and dropped frames. Back closes it.
  • Reload button — next to LIVE in the playback controls. Reopens the stream when a channel freezes or loses its sound (LIVE only jumps to the live point).
  • Double Press Back to Exit — Settings › General. Two quick presses of Back ask "Exit Hotdog TV?". While it's on, holding Back does the same as a short press.
  • Timeshift by storage size — Settings › Timeshift › Limit Buffer By › Storage Used, from 256 MB to 8 GB, instead of by time. Never uses more than 80% of the free space.
  • One-step network storage setup — SMB Connection Settings › Browse Network: pick the server, sign in if it asks, pick the share, then the folder. Saved and tested in one go.
  • Update prompt — shows a QR code for this page instead of a long list.

Improved

  • Movies and episodes buffer further ahead, so skipping forward and back no longer has to wait for the stream (going back replays from storage). The buffer stays within what the device's memory allows.
  • Series and Movies open quickly after the app starts. Updating playlists no longer holds everything else up, and a playlist that hasn't changed isn't rewritten at all.
  • The TV Guide stays open when you choose a channel — it plays in the preview. Change it in Settings › Appearance › TV Guide › Preview.
  • Playlists' own TV guides are picked up. A playlist that names its guide (x-tvg-url) has it added and downloaded, including playlists set up from the sync portal.
  • Home stops playback. Leaving the app with Home no longer keeps the channel playing in the background. It carries on when you come back.
  • Audio languages show their full name (English, not EN).
  • A TV guide that fails to download now says why.

Fixed

  • Channels stuck loading forever with no error after exiting and reopening the app.
  • The TV Guide opening on the previous channel after using Recent Channels.
  • Holding Back to exit could open Android's settings menu.
  • A freeze while watching some episodes.
  • The picture could stop after changing Buffer Size or Decoder while watching.
