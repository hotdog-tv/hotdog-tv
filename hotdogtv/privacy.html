Hotdog TV — Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

Hotdog TV is a personal IPTV/media player app (Android TV, desktop, Apple TV) built for use with a playlist or server you already have access to — such as an Xtream Codes, M3U, Jellyfin or Silo provider you supply yourself. This page explains what the app does with your data.

What the app does

  • Connects directly to the IPTV/media server address, username and password you enter. These credentials are stored only on your own device(s), never sent to us.
  • Optionally syncs settings, watch progress, favourites and recordings between your own devices via a sync server you run yourself (self-hosted, or one you otherwise control). We do not operate a central sync service that stores your data.
  • Looks up film/show titles, artwork and air dates from TMDB to show posters and guide information. Only the title/ids needed for that lookup are sent to TMDB — see TMDB's own privacy policy.
  • Looks up song lyrics from lrclib.net when showing synced lyrics for radio playback.
  • Checks for app updates against our own download server, and can fetch TV guide (EPG) data from the source your playlist provider or you configure.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • No account system, sign-up or login for the app itself.
  • We never see your IPTV provider credentials, viewing history or recordings — those stay between your device(s) and the server(s) you point them at.

Diagnostics you choose to send

The app has a "Report a Problem" feature. It only runs, and only sends anything, when you explicitly trigger it — nothing is collected automatically in the background. What you choose to send may include device/app version info, recent logs and a description you write, sent to our report endpoint for the sole purpose of fixing the issue you're reporting.

Children's privacy

Hotdog TV is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change too. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
